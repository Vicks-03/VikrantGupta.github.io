About Me

Projects & Reports

Detecting Bars in Galaxy Pairs and Comparing Their Properties with Isolated Galaxies

Summer Internship Report (IIA Bengaluru) July 2026

Summer research project conducted under Prof. Mousumi Das (IIA) and Ms. Saili Keshri. Analyzed 6,292 Arp-Madore galaxies, screening 372 candidates and using VISTA VIRCam Ks-band imaging with Photutils ellipse fitting to isolate bars, measure deprojected lengths, and compute stellar masses. Revealed an environmental split where dual-barred systems occur exclusively in major mergers (q ≥ 0.3).
